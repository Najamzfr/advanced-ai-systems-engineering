Vectorless document navigation uses metadata, lexical selection, and a document tree to choose sections before fetching full text. Chunkless means fixed chunks are not the primary navigation unit; it does not mean no structure, indexing, model calls, or cost. A tree node needs a title, child IDs, section span, and bounded navigation policy. Wrong headings and tables expose different failures than embedding search.

The required configurations are BM25-only, vector-only, hybrid, hybrid plus reranking, document-tree navigation, and long-context. These span four families: lexical, dense/hybrid, tree, and long-context. Freeze questions, corpus, generator, output limit, scope, and total retrieval/context budget. Long-context must use a deployable document-selection policy shared with other systems; do not grant it the gold document alone. An oracle-context run is a separately labeled diagnostic for generator error, not a competing deployable system.
