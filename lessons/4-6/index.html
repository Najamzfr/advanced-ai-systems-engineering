The release integrates normalized sources, six retrieval configurations, scoped MCP, bounded planning, an evidence ledger, independent verification, and a cited report. Keep 80, 100 evaluation questions with QASPER provenance and separately labeled multi-source/adversarial additions. Basic API, trace, configuration, and deployment behavior comes from Week 3.

A defense connects every architectural choice to a failure or measured gain. Demonstrate one supported answer, one contradiction, one absent-answer case, and one denied or failed tool. Reproduce the evaluation from a clean checkout. Report unsupported claims and uncertainty honestly; a plausible demo is not a substitute for the held-out evaluation. Use this week's assignment as the project review so the learner writes one coherent report.

Cheat sheet

Project 1 release and technical defense

Definition

A research system plans work, collects evidence, checks claims and produces a cited report.

Intuition

Every material claim needs a traceable source and a support decision.

Pattern

Complete the Project 1 demo and defense, then carry the evaluated tool and state primitives into the customer platform.

Common mistake

A real citation can still refer to the wrong revision or fail to support the claim.

Production notes

Freeze evaluation questions and preserve evidence, contradictions and failed runs.
