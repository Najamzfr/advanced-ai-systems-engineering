The final report covers problem, requirements, data limitations, architecture, components, agent/tool contracts, evaluation, experiments, security, reliability, observability, deployment, performance, and future work. Use measured evidence and precise denominators. Distinguish offline purchase prediction, reviewer acceptance, operational task completion, and causal business impact.

The demo should show ingestion and cutoff-safe profiles, a grounded draft, a rejected invented claim, denied consent, exact approval, duplicate-event handling, crash recovery, a full trace, and rollback. Defend why an agent is needed, why each specialist exists, what remains deterministic, and what would make you remove complexity. A limitation stated clearly is stronger than an unmeasured scale claim.

A final table lists "150 scenarios defined, 150 replayed, 120 run with the selected model, 30 blocked by hardware/provider limits." This is honest but the missing model runs remain an unmet practical requirement; the course record should not certify them as executed.
