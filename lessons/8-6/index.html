A handover includes pinned dependencies, configuration examples without secrets, data manifests and licenses, fixture setup, test/evaluation commands, release identifiers, and a runbook. Reproduce from a clean environment with declared hardware and access. Verify that dashboards, traces, and rollback instructions refer to the same release.

Use the final review to distinguish activity completion from evidence. Watching a lecture, solving a practice case, passing an independent review, and demonstrating a reproducible artifact are different achievements. Keep the evidence path and reviewer notes in the course record. Export the local learning record before moving devices; course progress is not a hosted credential or automatic verification of the project.

The final evaluation is good, but rollback destroys pending approvals. Is the handover complete?

Read the explanation

No. State compatibility and pending workflows are part of release safety. Preserve or migrate approval state with explicit version checks, and demonstrate rollback without unauthorized effects.

Cheat sheet

Reproduce, review, and hand over

Definition

Production architecture defines data, execution, policy and recovery boundaries.

Intuition

A deployed model is one component of a service with operational constraints.

Pattern

Finish the final independent review, attach artifact evidence, export your record, and retain a prioritized limitations backlog.

Common mistake

A successful demo does not establish capacity or recovery behavior.

Production notes

Keep release gates, SLOs, canaries, rollback and incident evidence.
