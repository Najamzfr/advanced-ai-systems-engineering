Calculate cost per completed compliant task, including failed attempts, retrieval/indexing, training amortization, hosting, and human review. A cheaper answer that requires more correction may cost more operationally. Compare local and hosted models using utilization, hardware availability, privacy, maintenance, and workload variability; do not use a single token-price comparison.

Routing and escalation should be evaluated as complete policies. Compression can remove redundant prompt/evidence tokens but must preserve conditions and contradictions. Cache hits require compatible scope and freshness; measure invalidation and miss behavior. Sensitivity analysis varies request volume, output length, escalation fraction, and utilization so the decision is robust to plausible workload changes.

Cost accounting

Trade-off:

Quality
Keep the same acceptance criteria when comparing costs across systems.
Cost
Include retries, failed tasks, infrastructure and human review in the successful-task denominator.
Latency
Report user-visible percentiles and queueing under the same workload.

Study:

Small model routing
Send suitable tasks to a cheaper model only after slice-level evaluations show acceptable outcomes.
Escalation
Move a difficult or uncertain case to a stronger model or person under a defined policy.
Local vs hosted models
Compare measured serving cost, operations effort, data constraints and quality at the expected traffic level.
Caching
Reuse valid results under explicit scope, revision and expiry rules.
Prompt compression
Remove redundant prompt content while testing that required instructions and evidence remain effective.
Retrieval compression
Reduce selected evidence without losing sources, contradictions or facts needed for the answer.
Cost attribution
Assign expenses to a task, customer or experiment, including retries and failed attempts.

Work through an example

Template drafts cost little but need four minutes of review. An agent costs more in inference but reduces measured review to two minutes without increasing factual errors. The business case depends on representative review measurements and labor assumptions, not model cost alone.

Cheat sheet

Economics per successful task

Definition

A routing policy chooses a model using information available before the outcome is known.

Intuition

Measure the entire route, including retries and fallback calls.

Formula

Csuccess=Cinference+Cretrieval+Cretries+CoperationsNsuccessful tasksC_{\mathrm{success}}=\frac{C_{\mathrm{inference}}+C_{\mathrm{retrieval}}+C_{\mathrm{retries}}+C_{\mathrm{operations}}}{N_{\mathrm{successful\ tasks}}}

Compare cost under the same success criteria. A cheaper request can be more expensive per successful task.

Common mistake

Knowing two models' average accuracy does not identify failures at request time.

Production notes

Trace route decisions, cost per successful task and tail latency.
