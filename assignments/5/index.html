ASSIGNMENT 05 / WEEK 05

Incident Postmortem

Diagnose the regression across model, prompt, retrieval, data, tool, and orchestration layers.

Realistic scenario

Agent success rate fell from 91% to 68% after deployment.

Objective

Diagnose the regression across model, prompt, retrieval, data, tool, and orchestration layers.

Constraints

  • Distinguish observed evidence from hypotheses.
  • Preserve the original failing release and datasets for reproduction.
  • Do not change several components at once and declare a root cause.

Required work

  • Reconstruct a timestamped incident timeline and user impact.
  • Compare release, model, prompt, corpus, tool, and workflow versions.
  • Replay stratified failing cases against the prior version and isolate factors.
  • Specify mitigation, root cause, corrective actions, owners, and preventive controls.
Original curriculum brief

Incident Postmortem

Scenario:

Agent success rate fell from 91% to 68% after deployment.

Diagnose:

  • model
  • prompt
  • retrieval
  • data
  • tool
  • orchestration

Write:

  • incident timeline
  • evidence
  • root cause
  • corrective actions
  • preventive controls

Deliverables

  • Incident timeline
  • Evidence and root-cause analysis
  • Corrective-action table
  • New regression tests and detection plan

Submission format: a reproducible repository with the memo/report, raw evidence, diagrams, configurations, and a README linking each required item. The site tracks your completion; it does not upload or grade submissions.

Grading rubric

CriterionWeightEvidence for full credit
Problem framing and constraints15%Explicit workload, success criteria, and relevant assumptions.
Experimental evidence30%Reproducible comparisons, raw results, proper splits, and uncertainty.
Architecture and trade-offs25%Alternatives considered and a decision supported by measurements.
Failure, security, and reliability20%Real failure cases, enforceable controls, and recovery behavior.
Clarity and reproducibility10%Traceable evidence, readable diagrams, and complete reproduction steps.

Rubric weights are course extensions. Unreproducible results or missing security/evaluation evidence do not satisfy the canonical hard pass requirements.

Technical-defense questions

  • Which observation falsifies your root-cause hypothesis?
  • How do you distinguish data drift from a provider change?
  • What would your rollback preserve or invalidate?
