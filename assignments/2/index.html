ASSIGNMENT 02 / WEEK 02

RAG or Long Context?

Identify the evidence-based crossover between retrieval investment and long-context use.

Realistic scenario

A research team operates a growing document collection. Retrieval tuning has stalled while a larger-context model appears easier to maintain.

Objective

Identify the evidence-based crossover between retrieval investment and long-context use.

Constraints

  • Use the same held-out questions, corpus snapshot, and answer criteria.
  • Include corpus growth and update frequency; account for engineering complexity.
  • Do not use maximum advertised context length as a quality claim.

Required work

  • Use the five Lab 2 baselines and oracle-context diagnostic.
  • Vary corpus size, evidence position, relevance density, and query type.
  • Compare retrieval quality, context size, latency, cost, complexity, and failure modes.
  • Recommend a switching or routing policy with measurable thresholds.
Original curriculum brief

Research memo:

When should an engineering team stop improving RAG and use long context instead?

Discuss:

  • retrieval quality
  • context size
  • latency
  • cost
  • engineering complexity
  • failure modes

Deliverables

  • Research memo
  • Ablation table and paired uncertainty estimates
  • Failure taxonomy
  • Decision framework and reproducible experiment configuration

Submission format: a reproducible repository with the memo/report, raw evidence, diagrams, configurations, and a README linking each required item. The site tracks your completion; it does not upload or grade submissions.

Grading rubric

Rubric weights are course extensions. Unreproducible results or missing security/evaluation evidence do not satisfy the canonical hard pass requirements.

Technical-defense questions

  • What proves retrieval is the bottleneck?
  • How would frequent document updates change the choice?
  • Which queries still require lexical retrieval?
