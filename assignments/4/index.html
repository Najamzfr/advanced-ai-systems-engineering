ASSIGNMENT 04 / WEEK 04

Compound-System Architecture Review

Select the architecture to deploy and defend whether coordination creates measurable value.

Use this assignment as the Project 1 architecture review to avoid writing a duplicate report.

Realistic scenario

Project 1 has three working architectures, but the specialist system costs more and sometimes loses contradictory evidence.

Objective

Select the architecture to deploy and defend whether coordination creates measurable value.

Constraints

  • Use equal model, evidence, and budget conditions.
  • Compare single agent, planner–executor, and supervisor/specialists.
  • Retain contradictory evidence and abstention cases.

Required work

  • Analyze Lab 4 paired runs by difficulty and evidence requirements.
  • Quantify coordination overhead and categorize failed handoffs.
  • Ablate the verifier and shared context separately.
  • Write a deployment recommendation with acceptance and rollback gates.

Deliverables

  • Architecture review memo
  • Comparison table with uncertainty
  • Coordination failure traces
  • Project 1 release decision

Submission format: a reproducible repository with the memo/report, raw evidence, diagrams, configurations, and a README linking each required item. The site tracks your completion; it does not upload or grade submissions.

Grading rubric

CriterionWeightEvidence for full credit
Problem framing and constraints15%Explicit workload, success criteria, and relevant assumptions.
Experimental evidence30%Reproducible comparisons, raw results, proper splits, and uncertainty.
Architecture and trade-offs25%Alternatives considered and a decision supported by measurements.
Failure, security, and reliability20%Real failure cases, enforceable controls, and recovery behavior.
Clarity and reproducibility10%Traceable evidence, readable diagrams, and complete reproduction steps.

Rubric weights are course extensions. Unreproducible results or missing security/evaluation evidence do not satisfy the canonical hard pass requirements.

Technical-defense questions

  • Which improvement disappears under equal budgets?
  • Can a verifier share a model without being an independent check?
  • What evidence would make you remove a specialist?
