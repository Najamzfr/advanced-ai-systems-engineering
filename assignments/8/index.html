ASSIGNMENT 08 / WEEK 08

Production Defense Dossier

Defend the final system with reliability, security, performance, and economic evidence.

Use the Customer Intelligence & Campaign Operations Platform as your final defense. This assignment is the report review, not an additional report.

Realistic scenario

Your Project 2 platform faces a release review after a dependency outage and a request to halve operating cost.

Objective

Defend the final system with reliability, security, performance, and economic evidence.

Constraints

  • Use the canonical 150-scenario evaluation and 10–15 page Project 2 report.
  • Show limitations and failed experiments, not only the winning configuration.
  • All consequential actions require explicit approval.

Original brief (reference only)

  • Organize architecture, evaluation, security, telemetry, and chaos-test evidence.
  • Compare the baseline with a cost-reduction candidate on the same tasks.
  • Run a canary/rollback demonstration and explain every required oral-exam decision.
  • Prepare a concise demo and a trace-backed defense of failure recovery.

Deliverables

  • Final technical report
  • Evidence appendix and reproducible repository
  • Demo script
  • Oral-defense notes and limitations

Submission format: a reproducible repository with the memo/report, raw evidence, diagrams, configurations, and a README linking each required item. The site tracks your completion; it does not upload or grade submissions.

Grading rubric

CriterionWeightEvidence for full credit
Problem framing and constraints15%Explicit workload, success criteria, and relevant assumptions.
Experimental evidence30%Reproducible comparisons, raw results, proper splits, and uncertainty.
Architecture and trade-offs25%Alternatives considered and a decision supported by measurements.
Failure, security, and reliability20%Real failure cases, enforceable controls, and recovery behavior.
Clarity and reproducibility10%Traceable evidence, readable diagrams, and complete reproduction steps.

Rubric weights are course extensions. Unreproducible results or missing security/evaluation evidence do not satisfy the canonical hard pass requirements.

Technical-defense questions

  • How would you halve cost with minimal quality loss?
  • Which failure requires abstention rather than fallback?
  • What measurement would invalidate your architecture choice?
