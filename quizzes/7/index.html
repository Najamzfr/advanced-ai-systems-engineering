Reasoning under real constraints

QUESTION 01 / 06

What makes workflow execution durable?

Review guidance

Persist state/checkpoints and resume after process death without assuming effects did not occur.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 02 / 06

Assume at-least-once delivery; deduplicate logical effects with stable keys.

QUESTION 03 / 06

Difference between retry and compensation?

Review guidance

Retry repeats an attempt; compensation offsets an already-completed business effect.
