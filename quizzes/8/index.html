WEEK 08 / INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENT

Defend your systems judgment.

Eight unseen scenarios. Draft your answers before revealing review criteria. These open-ended answers require self, peer, or instructor review.

Diagnostic quiz

Complete four automatically checked diagnostics, then write your answers to the eight independent scenarios below. The written defenses use a separate review rubric.

Architecture choice

Offline customer ranking predicts future purchases. What can you conclude about campaign uplift?

Multi-select

A safe response-cache key may need which inputs?

Sequencing

Order a controlled improvement cycle.

Matching

Match dependency failure to a defensible behavior.

This page saves drafts and rubric ratings; it does not automatically grade technical reasoning. Score each response 0 (missing/incorrect), 1 (partial), 2 (sound with a gap), or 3 (sound and justified). A suggested readiness threshold is 18/24, with no unresolved authorization or evidence-integrity errors. Ratings are self-reported unless you record an external reviewer.
SCENARIO 01 / 08

A fallback provider receives all traffic during an outage and collapses. What was missing from the reliability design?

Reveal the review criteria

Capacity/headroom/admission for fallback, retry budget/circuit breaker, load shedding and degraded response policy; availability of a URL is not tested resilience.

Review unit 8-1: Reliability under overload and dependency failure →

SCENARIO 02 / 08

Can adding the p95 latency of each serial component predict the request p95? Explain a better method.

Reveal the review criteria

Not generally; distributions/correlation and queueing matter; measure end-to-end representative traces/load and inspect component contributions.

Review unit 8-1: Reliability under overload and dependency failure →

SCENARIO 03 / 08

A routing policy cuts model cost by 30% but doubles review time. Build the comparison the business actually needs.

Reveal the review criteria

Cost per successful compliant task including review labor, failures, retrieval/hosting and quality; representative measured review instructions and sensitivity assumptions.

Review unit 8-2: Economics per successful task →

SCENARIO 04 / 08

A release is tuned repeatedly on the same final test set after every production-like failure. What evaluation process should replace it?

Reveal the review criteria

Use development/regression sets for iteration, preserve a fresh held-out final set, version cases and decisions, run paired gates and controlled canary/rollback.

Review unit 8-3: Close the telemetry-to-evaluation loop →

SCENARIO 05 / 08

Of 150 scenarios, 15 safely escalate and 135 complete. How should success and safety be reported?

Reveal the review criteria

Separate expected escalation correctness, business task completion, policy compliance and category denominators; do not call all escalation failure or all safe failure business success.

Review unit 8-4: The final chaos and evaluation matrix →

SCENARIO 06 / 08

An injected database outage produces zero unauthorized effects but loses pending jobs. Which reliability property failed?

Reveal the review criteria

Durability/recoverability and liveness; persist accepted work, reconcile states, test restart and recovery time alongside security invariants.

Review unit 8-4: The final chaos and evaluation matrix →

SCENARIO 07 / 08

A final report claims customer uplift from historical transactions without campaign exposures. Rewrite the measurable outcome.

Reveal the review criteria

Use cutoff-safe offline ranking, numeric/grounding accuracy, reviewer time/acceptance and operational compliance; causal uplift requires controlled treatment evidence.

Review unit 8-5: Report, demo, and architecture defense →

SCENARIO 08 / 08

Another engineer can run the demo but cannot reproduce evaluations or rollback. What remains before artifact verification?

Reveal the review criteria

Pinned inputs/configurations/evaluators, clean-run results, failure evidence and state-compatible rollback/runbook; demo alone is not reproducible engineering evidence.

Original curriculum defense prompts

  1. RAG or long context?
  2. Workflow or agent?
  3. One agent or multiple agents?
  4. Prompting or fine-tuning?
  5. Hosted or local model?
  6. How do you know retrieval is the bottleneck?
  7. How should agents be evaluated?
  8. How do you secure tool use?
  9. How do you detect production drift?
  10. How would you halve cost with minimal quality loss?

Review and next action

Independent review: not recorded

Revisit any weak unit, revise your answer, and ask a peer to challenge the assumptions. Retaking the same questions is review practice, not a fresh unseen test.

Artifact evidence · self-reported

Record the repository/release, reproduction command, result location and reviewer. This course does not inspect your external repository or certify these claims.
