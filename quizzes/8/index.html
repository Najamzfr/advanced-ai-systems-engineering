QUIZ 08 / WEEK 08

Oral systems exam

Write your reasoning, assumptions, and evidence. There are no multiple-choice shortcuts.

Drafts save in this browser. Review guidance is for self-assessment, not an automated grade. The ten canonical oral-exam prompts are preserved below.
QUESTION 01 / 10

RAG or long context?

Review guidance

Compare retrieval quality, corpus size/freshness, cost, and evidence provenance.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 02 / 10

Workflow or agent?

Review guidance

Use task variability and controlled transitions; justify dynamic decisions.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 03 / 10

One agent or multiple agents?

Review guidance

Compare paired outcomes and coordination overhead under equal budgets.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 04 / 10

Prompting or fine-tuning?

Review guidance

Distinguish behavior failures from missing facts and test simpler interventions.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 05 / 10

Hosted or local model?

Review guidance

State privacy, hardware, latency, reliability, and cost assumptions.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 06 / 10

How do you know retrieval is the bottleneck?

Review guidance

Use oracle context, retrieval labels, and fixed-generator ablations.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 07 / 10

How should agents be evaluated?

Review guidance

Measure intermediate contracts and final task outcomes separately.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 08 / 10

How do you secure tool use?

Review guidance

Enforce typed calls, authorization, least privilege, approval, and audit.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 09 / 10

How do you detect production drift?

Review guidance

Compare versioned slices and telemetry; review new failure cases without contaminating the release set.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 10 / 10

How would you halve cost with minimal quality loss?

Review guidance

Measure routing, caching, compression, and smaller-model candidates on a quality–cost frontier.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

Completion review

  • Answer every question before reviewing the guidance.
  • Revise any answer that states a preference without a mechanism or experiment.
  • Use the corresponding lab results as evidence; note any uncertainty.
