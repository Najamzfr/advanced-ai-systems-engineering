QUIZ 04 / WEEK 04

Reasoning under real constraints

Write your reasoning, assumptions, and evidence. There are no multiple-choice shortcuts.

Drafts save in this browser. Review guidance is for self-assessment, not an automated grade. These questions extend the canonical planning and multi-agent objectives.
QUESTION 01 / 06

A specialist system improves success by 2 percentage points but triples token use. What experiment determines whether to deploy it?

Review guidance

Use paired held-out cases, uncertainty, equal budgets, and task difficulty slices.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 02 / 06

Two workers return contradictory claims from different dates. Design the evidence merge and verification behavior.

Review guidance

Retain source dates, provenance, and contradictory evidence; do not vote away conflicts.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 03 / 06

A planner repeatedly adds tasks after a tool fails. Identify the missing controls and termination policy.

Review guidance

Bound steps, replans, budget, and repeated failures; persist stop reasons.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 04 / 06

A supervisor waits forever for one worker. Describe timeout, partial-result, and escalation semantics.

Review guidance

Use deadlines, cancellation, explicit missing-result state, and safe escalation.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 05 / 06

How would you isolate whether improvement comes from more inference calls or specialization?

Review guidance

Equalize calls or budgets and ablate roles while keeping tools and evidence fixed.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

QUESTION 06 / 06

A verifier shares all the writer’s context. What correlated errors can remain and how would you measure them?

Review guidance

Inspect shared blind spots, independent evidence checks, and human-adjudicated disagreements.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?

Completion review

  • Answer every question before reviewing the guidance.
  • Revise any answer that states a preference without a mechanism or experiment.
  • Use the corresponding lab results as evidence; note any uncertainty.
