WEEK 04 / INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENT

Defend your systems judgment.

Eight unseen scenarios. Draft your answers before revealing review criteria. These open-ended answers require self, peer, or instructor review.

Diagnostic quiz

Complete four automatically checked diagnostics, then write your answers to the eight independent scenarios below. The written defenses use a separate review rubric.

Architecture choice

A three-agent system gains 2 points in task success but uses three times the tokens. What is the next useful experiment?

Multi-select

Two credible sources disagree. What should the evidence merge preserve?

Sequencing

Order a plan task with verification.

Matching

Match design to its distinguishing feature.

This page saves drafts and rubric ratings; it does not automatically grade technical reasoning. Score each response 0 (missing/incorrect), 1 (partial), 2 (sound with a gap), or 3 (sound and justified). A suggested readiness threshold is 18/24, with no unresolved authorization or evidence-integrity errors. Ratings are self-reported unless you record an external reviewer.
SCENARIO 01 / 08

A plan has two independent searches and a synthesis task that depends on only one. What validation and execution problem can follow?

Reveal the review criteria

Synthesis may run before needed evidence; acceptance/dependency schema must require both, schedule independent searches concurrently, and explicitly handle missing results.

SCENARIO 02 / 08

After each replan the runtime restores a fresh 10-call budget. Explain the exploit and correct state model.

Reveal the review criteria

Unlimited work via repeated replans; keep a global monotonic budget and bounded replan count; persist plan revisions/reasons without resetting consumed resources.

Review unit 4-1: Plans as dependency graphs →

SCENARIO 03 / 08

A claim is true in one source and contradicted by a newer revision. Define a verifier result and writer behavior.

Reveal the review criteria

Preserve both revisions and temporal scope; classify contradicted/partial as appropriate; cite evidence and explain scope; writer must not upgrade to universal support.

Review unit 4-2: Planner–executor–verifier contracts →

SCENARIO 04 / 08

A supervisor passes the full customer database to every specialist. Redesign agent-as-tool contracts.

Reveal the review criteria

Pass only necessary scoped facts/IDs, typed requests/results, limited capabilities and budgets; keep deterministic analytics behind tools and finalization with one owner.

Review unit 4-3: Six multi-agent arrangements →

SCENARIO 05 / 08

Choose between a blackboard and sequential handoff when specialists need shared evolving evidence. Name a failure introduced by your choice.

Reveal the review criteria

Justify shared structured state versus ordered context; blackboard needs ownership/version/merge rules; handoff can bottleneck or amplify errors; preserve provenance in either choice.

Review unit 4-3: Six multi-agent arrangements →

SCENARIO 06 / 08

One of three parallel workers times out after finding unique contradictory evidence. The merger drops its partial output and says consensus. Repair this behavior.

Reveal the review criteria

Persist partial evidence, mark worker incomplete, preserve contradiction, apply explicit completion policy and deadline; missing output cannot imply agreement.

Review unit 4-4: Coordination failure and recovery →

SCENARIO 07 / 08

The planner–verifier system gets 88 correct and the single agent 86 correct on the same 100 cases. What else is needed before adding the complexity?

Reveal the review criteria

Paired win/loss cases, uncertainty/slices, equal budgets, tokens/cost/tail latency, unsupported-claim change and coordination failures; two-point aggregate alone is insufficient.

Review unit 4-5: Compare architectures under equal budgets →

SCENARIO 08 / 08

Your research project passes 90 direct questions but has no absent-answer or adversarial cases. Assess it against the 80–100 question project contract.

Reveal the review criteria

Count alone is insufficient; preserve required category coverage and source-disjoint evaluation with labeled additions; add held-out missing-evidence/attack cases and rerun honestly.
