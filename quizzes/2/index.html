Reasoning under real constraints

Write your reasoning, assumptions, and evidence. There are no multiple-choice shortcuts.

Drafts save in this browser. Review guidance is for self-assessment, not an automated grade. All canonical quiz questions are preserved below.
QUESTION 01 / 06

Why can embeddings retrieve semantically relevant but operationally irrelevant chunks?

Review guidance

Distinguish semantic similarity from identifiers, access, time, and operational constraints.

Self-review: does your answer explain the mechanism, acknowledge the trade-off, and propose verifiable evidence?
