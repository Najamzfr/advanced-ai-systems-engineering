EDITION 03

Course coverage and validation

The canonical curriculum remains the technical baseline; the authorized customer-intelligence project replaces the earlier finance project.

What improved

  • Expanded three brief lessons per week into 52 explicitly aligned learning units.
  • Required video assignments with direct recordings/lesson links and complete written instruction.
  • Visible canonical topics and research readings; six retrieval and six adaptation conditions required.
  • Ten guided labs with fixtures, intermediate outputs, hints and transfer tasks.
  • 64 unseen technical scenarios with saved responses and honest human-review rubrics.
  • Reading pages return to the exact teaching unit; course continuation follows the next unfinished activity.
  • Local progress export/import and separate artifact evidence notes.

Validation boundaries

Structural checks verify pages, links, content contracts and local learning interactions. The starter fixtures verify their deterministic examples. These checks do not prove student learning, live model/GPU performance, third-party video playback or completion times. External recordings have source links and written access routes; independent learner walkthrough and playback/segment review remain to be completed.
