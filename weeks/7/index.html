Week 7 cheat sheet

Production architecture

Definition

Production architecture defines data, execution, policy and recovery boundaries.

Intuition

A deployed model is one component of a service with operational constraints.

Pattern or formula

Keep release gates, SLOs, canaries, rollback and incident evidence.

Common mistake

A successful demo does not establish capacity or recovery behavior.

Production notes

Keep release gates, SLOs, canaries, rollback and incident evidence.

Durable workflow

Definition

A durable workflow records progress so interrupted work can resume after a failure.

Intuition

Recovery must reconcile external effects, not only restart computation.

Pattern or formula

effect_key=hash⁡(workflow_id,logical_action_id)\mathrm{effect\_key}=\operatorname{hash}(\mathrm{workflow\_id},\mathrm{logical\_action\_id})

Common mistake

Retrying a timed-out write can duplicate an effect that already happened.

Production notes

Use checkpoints, stable idempotency keys, bounded retries and compensation.

Distributed observability

Definition

Observability connects logs, metrics and traces to the system's requests and decisions.

Intuition

A trace should explain where time, cost and failure entered a request.

Pattern or formula

Record span links, revisions and policy outcomes while redacting secrets.

Common mistake

A missing parent-child link can make queueing or worker delays invisible.

Production notes

Record span links, revisions and policy outcomes while redacting secrets.

Customer intelligence system

Definition

A customer intelligence system turns permission-scoped data into reviewable recommendations.

Intuition

Prediction and an intervention's causal effect answer different questions.

Pattern or formula

Keep data lineage, deterministic calculations, approval records and recovery tests.

Common mistake

A sales correlation does not prove campaign uplift.

Production notes

Keep data lineage, deterministic calculations, approval records and recovery tests.
