Week 4 cheat sheet

Planner and executor

Definition

A planner defines bounded work; executors perform steps and return evidence for review.

Intuition

Dependencies and acceptance conditions make a plan executable.

Pattern or formula

Persist plan revisions, step state, evidence references and retry limits.

Common mistake

Parallel execution is invalid when a step depends on unfinished evidence.

Production notes

Persist plan revisions, step state, evidence references and retry limits.

Multi-agent system

Definition

A multi-agent system divides work among model-driven components with explicit handoffs.

Intuition

Coordination is another source of latency and failure.

Pattern or formula

Give each role a contract, owner, budget and observable failure path.

Common mistake

More agents can amplify the same error instead of providing independent verification.

Production notes

Give each role a contract, owner, budget and observable failure path.

Evaluation pipeline

Definition

An evaluation compares versioned outputs with a task-specific definition of success.

Intuition

Measure the component that could cause the observed failure.

Pattern or formula

Recall@K⁡=∣relevant∩top K∣∣relevant∣\operatorname{Recall@K}=\frac{|\mathrm{relevant}\cap\mathrm{top}\ K|}{|\mathrm{relevant}|}

Common mistake

A higher aggregate score can hide a regression on a critical slice.

Production notes

Freeze case IDs and report denominators, uncertainty and failed runs.

Research system

Definition

A research system plans work, collects evidence, checks claims and produces a cited report.

Intuition

Every material claim needs a traceable source and a support decision.

Pattern or formula

Freeze evaluation questions and preserve evidence, contradictions and failed runs.

Common mistake

A real citation can still refer to the wrong revision or fail to support the claim.

Production notes

Freeze evaluation questions and preserve evidence, contradictions and failed runs.
