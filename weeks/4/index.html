04 · Lectures / topics

LECTURE 4.1

Planning needs executable acceptance criteria

A plan needs typed tasks, dependencies, expected evidence, and completion checks. Replan on observed failure or changed evidence, not merely a new model opinion. Bound depth, calls, and wall-clock time.

LECTURE 4.2

Coordination has a measurable cost

Parallel workers reduce elapsed time only when tasks are sufficiently independent. Measure coordination calls, shared-state contention, contradictory outputs, and repeated retrieval. Compare architectures under equal model and token budgets.

LECTURE 4.3

Verification must be independent of synthesis

A verifier labels claim support against evidence; a writer organizes the report. Keep their roles separate and pass structured verdicts. Count unsupported material claims and omitted contradictory evidence, not just prose quality.
