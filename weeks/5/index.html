Week 5 cheat sheet

Evaluation pipeline

Definition

An evaluation compares versioned outputs with a task-specific definition of success.

Intuition

Measure the component that could cause the observed failure.

Pattern or formula

Recall@K⁡=∣relevant∩top K∣∣relevant∣\operatorname{Recall@K}=\frac{|\mathrm{relevant}\cap\mathrm{top}\ K|}{|\mathrm{relevant}|}

Common mistake

A higher aggregate score can hide a regression on a critical slice.

Production notes

Freeze case IDs and report denominators, uncertainty and failed runs.

Trust boundary

Definition

A trust boundary separates data the system may inspect from instructions it is authorized to execute.

Intuition

Retrieved documents and tool output remain untrusted data.

Pattern or formula

ASR=Nattacks achieving the forbidden objectiveNattempted attacks\mathrm{ASR}=\frac{N_{\mathrm{attacks\ achieving\ the\ forbidden\ objective}}}{N_{\mathrm{attempted\ attacks}}}

Common mistake

A safe final answer does not cancel an unauthorized tool effect.

Production notes

Enforce least privilege, human approval and output checks at execution time.

Distributed observability

Definition

Observability connects logs, metrics and traces to the system's requests and decisions.

Intuition

A trace should explain where time, cost and failure entered a request.

Pattern or formula

Record span links, revisions and policy outcomes while redacting secrets.

Common mistake

A missing parent-child link can make queueing or worker delays invisible.

Production notes

Record span links, revisions and policy outcomes while redacting secrets.

Customer intelligence system

Definition

A customer intelligence system turns permission-scoped data into reviewable recommendations.

Intuition

Prediction and an intervention's causal effect answer different questions.

Pattern or formula

Keep data lineage, deterministic calculations, approval records and recovery tests.

Common mistake

A sales correlation does not prove campaign uplift.

Production notes

Keep data lineage, deterministic calculations, approval records and recovery tests.
