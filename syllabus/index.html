The engineering standard.

A graduate-level independent course: implement primitives, measure alternatives, and defend a reproducible system.

Before you begin

  • Python, APIs, Git and Docker fundamentals.
  • Embeddings, basic retrieval-augmented generation and elementary evaluation.
  • Basic probability/statistics and linear algebra for metrics and inference accounting.
  • SQL or pandas for deterministic customer analytics; access to suitable compute for declared real experiments.

How to use the course

Follow lessons in order. Each lesson starts with its assigned or companion video, then provides an explanation, a focused visual, reading, exercise and quiz. Review the exercise and pass the quiz to complete the lesson. Review book and paper sections in the course reader; each reading has its own completion checkbox.

10. Hard Pass Requirements

A learner cannot pass the course if:

  • no automated evaluation exists
  • projects cannot be reproduced
  • agent tools are unrestricted
  • the system has no tests
  • no observability exists
  • architecture decisions have no evidence
  • projects cannot handle failures
  • security testing is missing
  • the final system cannot explain limitations

Required experimental scope

Required comparison contract. Retrieval has six configurations across four families: BM25-only, vector-only, hybrid without reranking, hybrid plus reranking, vectorless document-tree navigation, and long-context. Adaptation has six conditions: zero-shot, few-shot, advanced prompting, RAG, LoRA, and LoRA plus RAG. Freeze evaluation and budgets; larger sweeps are extensions, but these conditions remain required.

Project 1 retains 80–100 questions and three architecture comparisons. Week 5 retains at least 50 attacks. Project 2 retains 150 scenarios and the final 10–15 page report. Week 6 retains the 100 requests/second, p95-under-two-seconds capacity target as a proposal to substantiate, not an assumed result.

What progress means

Completion checkboxes and open-response ratings are self-reported. Independent questions are distinct from lesson practice, but require human judgment. Live model execution, GPU performance, and project reproducibility must be supported by your evidence; replay and blocked experiments are labeled separately.
